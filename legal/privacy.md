## What we collect
- **Account data:** email address, name if you give it, and billing details handled by Stripe (we never see or store full card numbers).
- **Test data:** the URLs you test, verification tokens, screenshots, console and network errors, load-test measurements, and the reports we generate. Screenshots can include anything your site displays to a new visitor.
- **Usage data:** basic logs (IP address, browser, pages used) for security and debugging.

## How we use it
To run the tests you request, produce reports, bill you, prevent abuse (for example checking that you control the sites you test), and improve the product. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## Service providers (processors)
- Anthropic: AI model that drives the test browser and writes fix prompts (page content and errors are sent to it).
- Fly.io: servers that run browsers and load generators.
- Cloudflare: hosting, storage of screenshots and reports, email routing for test inboxes.
- Stripe: payments.
- Optional, only if you connect them: Slack and Linear, to send you results.

## Retention
Run data (screenshots, logs, measurements) is deleted after 30 days unless you save the report. You can delete any report or your whole account at any time from settings or by emailing us. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and object to certain processing. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We answer within 30 days.

## Security
Reports are private by default and shared only through links you create. Keys you give us (for example a restricted read-only Stripe key) are encrypted at rest, used only for the run you started, and deleted after it.
